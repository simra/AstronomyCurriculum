Physical and mathematical model

State what is being evolved

Bayes' rule combines likelihood and prior to produce a posterior. For binomial completeness data, a Beta prior gives a conjugate Beta posterior; posterior quantiles describe parameter uncertainty conditional on the assumed Bernoulli model and prior.

\[p(\theta\mid D)\propto p(D\mid\theta)p(\theta),\quad p\mid k,n\sim\mathrm{Beta}(k+\alpha,n-k+\beta)\]

Define the state, coordinates, units, initial/boundary conditions, and domain before interpreting solver output.

Numerical method

Algorithm choice is a modeling decision

Write the likelihood and prior; check support and identifiability; sample with an explicit generator seed; compare Monte Carlo quantiles to analytic conjugate summaries; separate posterior Monte Carlo error from scientific uncertainty.

  1. Write the model and its assumptions.
  2. Choose a solver and identify its stability, accuracy, and conservation properties.
  3. Record configuration, tolerances or timestep, and output sampling.
  4. Compare against an analytic limit, independent method, or controlled refinement.

Verification

What would falsify this result?

  • Repeat with a smaller timestep, tighter tolerance, or finer spatial grid.
  • Track conservation or residual structure in addition to visual agreement.
  • Check units, coordinate frame, boundary conditions, and invariants independently.
  • Change a random seed when the method is stochastic; report the resulting sampling uncertainty.

Do not report a smoother curve as proof of convergence.
