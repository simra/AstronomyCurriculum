Physical and mathematical model

State what is being evolved

An ODE solver advances a state by approximating a local trajectory. Adaptive Runge-Kutta methods estimate error from embedded formulas, but tolerances constrain an error estimator rather than guaranteeing a global physical error bound.

\[\dot{\mathbf y}=\mathbf f(t,\mathbf y),\quad e_{\rm local}\leq atol+rtol\,|\mathbf y|\]

Define the state, coordinates, units, initial/boundary conditions, and domain before interpreting solver output.

Verification

What would falsify this result?

  • Repeat with a smaller timestep, tighter tolerance, or finer spatial grid.
  • Track conservation or residual structure in addition to visual agreement.
  • Check units, coordinate frame, boundary conditions, and invariants independently.
  • Change a random seed when the method is stochastic; report the resulting sampling uncertainty.

Do not report a smoother curve as proof of convergence.

Failure modes
