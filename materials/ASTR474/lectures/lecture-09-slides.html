Motivation

How many photon histories are required to resolve a transmission probability?

Monte Carlo estimates expectations by sampling a probability law. Independent Bernoulli transmission trials have standard error approximately sqrt[p(1-p)/N], so precision improves only as N^-1/2.

Today’s result must be interpretable as a physical statement with declared data, algorithm, uncertainty, and a check that could fail.

Verification

What would falsify this result?

  • Repeat with a smaller timestep, tighter tolerance, or finer spatial grid.
  • Track conservation or residual structure in addition to visual agreement.
  • Check units, coordinate frame, boundary conditions, and invariants independently.
  • Change a random seed when the method is stochastic; report the resulting sampling uncertainty.

Do not report a smoother curve as proof of convergence.
