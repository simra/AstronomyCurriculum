Scoring overview

Problems 1–4 are worth 25 points each. Award credit for correct reasoning, units, reproducibility, and interpretation; separate arithmetic slips from conceptual errors.

Common errors

  • mixing kpc, pc, and meters;
  • reversing a comparative ratio;
  • calling a model-dependent mass a direct measurement;
  • omitting selection effects or uncertainty.
