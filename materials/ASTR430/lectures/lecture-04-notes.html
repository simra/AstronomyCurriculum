ASTR 430 Lecture 04: Identifiers and Cross-Matches

Research Methods in Astronomy · study notes

Learning objectives

  • Cross-match catalogs while quantifying false matches and selection effects.
  • Explain coordinates, angular separation, likelihood ratios, and background density with the formal object.
  • Separate evidence, transformation, inference, and limitation.

Terms Developed in Context

These terms form the lecture's reasoning chain from observable through model to a bounded physical conclusion.

Sky coordinate

A position on the celestial sphere expressed in a declared reference frame and epoch. In this lecture it supports the evidence-to-model chain for coordinates, angular separation, likelihood ratios, and background density.

Use and relationship: Read it together with False-match rate and Angular separation. The governing relation \(w=exp(-r^2/(2 sigma_r^2))/rho\) shows where the concept enters quantitatively; the student should state whether its value is measured, calibrated, adopted, or inferred.

Boundary: This is not a posterior match probability until background density and competing candidates are included. This caveat prevents the term from being treated as a unique explanation or a context-free number.

Method in context

A cross-match is inference under a background model, not a string join on object names. Ask what a second researcher needs to reproduce the claim: raw input, processing state, software or database version, and inferential step.

Derivation and assumptions

\(w=exp(-r^2/(2 sigma_r^2))/rho\)

Define every symbol and explain the failure mode. A method becomes scientific when assumptions are visible enough to be tested.

Worked example

exp[-0.5(0.8/0.3)^2] = 0.0285655.

This is not a posterior match probability until background density and competing candidates are included. Require an independent recomputation and one perturbation.

Misconceptions and study guidance

Do not confuse a data service with a measurement, a catalog identifier with physical identity, a formal error with total uncertainty, or a polished figure with a validated inference. Reconstruct the evidence chain before interpreting.
