ASTR 430 Lecture 11: Statistical Ethics and Inference

Research Methods in Astronomy · study notes

Learning objectives

  • Recognize p-hacking, multiple comparisons, leakage, and uncertainty laundering.
  • Explain pre-registration, multiplicity, train-test separation, and effect sizes with the formal object.
  • Separate evidence, transformation, inference, and limitation.

Method in context

Statistical ethics demands disclosure of search degrees of freedom and the full analysis path. Ask what a second researcher needs to reproduce the claim: raw input, processing state, software or database version, and inferential step.

Derivation and assumptions

\(p_adjusted=min(1,m p)\)

Define every symbol and explain the failure mode. A method becomes scientific when assumptions are visible enough to be tested.

Worked example

MAST serves mission products, Gaia astrometric catalogs, SDSS imaging/spectroscopy, and Exoplanet Archive curated tables; product scope is the discriminant.

Archive choice is part of scientific design. Require an independent recomputation and one perturbation.

Misconceptions and study guidance

Do not confuse a data service with a measurement, a catalog identifier with physical identity, a formal error with total uncertainty, or a polished figure with a validated inference. Reconstruct the evidence chain before interpreting.
