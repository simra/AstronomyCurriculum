ASTR 430 Lecture 08: Scientific Visualization and Figure Critique

Research Methods in Astronomy · study notes

Learning objectives

  • Critique a figure for encoding, uncertainty, accessibility, and claim scope.
  • Explain scales, colormaps, uncertainty displays, annotations, and captions with the formal object.
  • Separate evidence, transformation, inference, and limitation.

Terms Developed in Context

These terms form the lecture's reasoning chain from observable through model to a bounded physical conclusion.

Visual encoding

The mapping of data variables to position, length, color, shape, or other graphical channels. In this lecture it supports the evidence-to-model chain for scales, colormaps, uncertainty displays, annotations, and captions.

Use and relationship: Read it together with Scientific caption and Color map. The governing relation \(information density=encoded variables/visual channels\) shows where the concept enters quantitatively; the student should state whether its value is measured, calibrated, adopted, or inferred.

Boundary: A plot must disclose transformations, units, limits, and uncertainty encodings. This caveat prevents the term from being treated as a unique explanation or a context-free number.

Method in context

A figure is an argument with visual premises: the caption must say what is measured, transformed, and model-dependent. Ask what a second researcher needs to reproduce the claim: raw input, processing state, software or database version, and inferential step.

Derivation and assumptions

\(information density=encoded variables/visual channels\)

Define every symbol and explain the failure mode. A method becomes scientific when assumptions are visible enough to be tested.

Worked example

A log-log slope is d log(y)/d log(x); changing axes changes visual sensitivity, not the data.

A plot must disclose transformations, units, limits, and uncertainty encodings. Require an independent recomputation and one perturbation.

Misconceptions and study guidance

Do not confuse a data service with a measurement, a catalog identifier with physical identity, a formal error with total uncertainty, or a polished figure with a validated inference. Reconstruct the evidence chain before interpreting.
