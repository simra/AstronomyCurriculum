ASTR 430 Lecture 12: Conference-Style Communication

Research Methods in Astronomy · study notes

Learning objectives

  • Build a concise narrative from question through evidence to limitation.
  • Explain abstracts, posters, talks, captions, and questions with the formal object.
  • Separate evidence, transformation, inference, and limitation.

Method in context

Communication is part of reproducibility when the audience can identify evidence, uncertainty, and claim boundary. Ask what a second researcher needs to reproduce the claim: raw input, processing state, software or database version, and inferential step.

Derivation and assumptions

\(message=question+evidence+uncertainty+consequence\)

Define every symbol and explain the failure mode. A method becomes scientific when assumptions are visible enough to be tested.

Worked example

1-(1-0.05)^20 = 0.641514.

The calculation assumes independent tests; undisclosed multiplicity still matters. Require an independent recomputation and one perturbation.

Misconceptions and study guidance

Do not confuse a data service with a measurement, a catalog identifier with physical identity, a formal error with total uncertainty, or a polished figure with a validated inference. Reconstruct the evidence chain before interpreting.
