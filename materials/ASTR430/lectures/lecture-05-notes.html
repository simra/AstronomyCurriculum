ASTR 430 Lecture 05: Uncertainty Propagation and Calibration

Research Methods in Astronomy · study notes

Learning objectives

  • Propagate measurement and calibration uncertainty without hiding covariance.
  • Explain Jacobians, covariance, systematic offsets, and calibration transfer with the formal object.
  • Separate evidence, transformation, inference, and limitation.

Method in context

A small formal error does not certify accuracy when a calibration term is shared across a sample. Ask what a second researcher needs to reproduce the claim: raw input, processing state, software or database version, and inferential step.

Derivation and assumptions

\(C_y=J C_x J^T+C_sys\)

Define every symbol and explain the failure mode. A method becomes scientific when assumptions are visible enough to be tested.

Worked example

For d=1/p, d=0.5 kpc and sigma_d≈0.0125 kpc; the stated zero point gives d=0.495786 kpc.

The propagation is local; the offset is shared and is not independent noise. Require an independent recomputation and one perturbation.

Misconceptions and study guidance

Do not confuse a data service with a measurement, a catalog identifier with physical identity, a formal error with total uncertainty, or a polished figure with a validated inference. Reconstruct the evidence chain before interpreting.
