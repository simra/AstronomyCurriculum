ASTR 430 Lecture 02: Literature Search as an Experiment

Research Methods in Astronomy · study notes

Learning objectives

  • Construct and freeze a literature search another researcher can rerun.
  • Explain ADS query syntax, scope, screening, and inclusion criteria with the formal object.
  • Separate evidence, transformation, inference, and limitation.

Method in context

The literature is data: query string, timestamp, database, and screening rule are part of the measurement. Ask what a second researcher needs to reproduce the claim: raw input, processing state, software or database version, and inferential step.

Derivation and assumptions

\(P(keep|paper)=P(title,abstract,methods|rule)\)

Define every symbol and explain the failure mode. A method becomes scientific when assumptions are visible enough to be tested.

Worked example

A frozen screen retaining 6 of 18 records has fraction 6/18 = 0.333333.

This describes the screen, not the quality of the literature. Require an independent recomputation and one perturbation.

Misconceptions and study guidance

Do not confuse a data service with a measurement, a catalog identifier with physical identity, a formal error with total uncertainty, or a polished figure with a validated inference. Reconstruct the evidence chain before interpreting.
