3. Uncertainty budget

σ_total = √(1.71² + 1.32²) = √(2.92 + 1.7424) = 2.16 ADU. The two terms are comparable (within about 30% of each other), so neither cleanly dominates; this indicates the calibration-floor noise on this camera comes from a mix of read noise (captured by the bias master) and residual dark-frame noise (captured by the matched-exposure dark master), not from one overwhelming source.
