3. Cadence adequacy

Samples across event = 3.4 h / 0.75 h = 4.5. About 5 samples is borderline: enough to detect the event but only marginally adequate to resolve ingress/egress shape in detail; a finer cadence would be preferable.

4. Nyquist-style check

Recovering a 5.2 h period needs a cadence well under half the period, i.e., well under 2.6 h. A 6 h cadence is coarser than that limit and risks aliasing. A 1 h cadence satisfies Δt ≪ T and would adequately sample the periodic signal.
