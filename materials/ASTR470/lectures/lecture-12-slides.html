Failure modes and limits

  • A fitted temperature is not automatically a local thermodynamic temperature.
  • A mission bandpass is not the same as effective area or sensitivity.
  • A simple model can establish a scale without proving a unique physical cause.
  • Selection and background can change the apparent population or transient rate.

Observational signature

Ask what the detector actually records: photon energy, arrival time, position, or a particle direction. The source interpretation is credible only when the signature survives background subtraction, calibration choices, and a plausible alternative model.
