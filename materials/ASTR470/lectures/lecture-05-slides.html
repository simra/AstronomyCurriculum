Failure modes and limits

  • A fitted temperature is not automatically a local thermodynamic temperature.
  • A mission bandpass is not the same as effective area or sensitivity.
  • A simple model can establish a scale without proving a unique physical cause.
  • Selection and background can change the apparent population or transient rate.

Active reasoning

Prompt: Which additional observable would distinguish the leading interpretation from the most plausible alternative? State the measurement, expected direction, and uncertainty that would matter.

Synthesis

  • High-energy photons and particles encode extreme temperature, gravity, magnetic field, and timescale.
  • The inference chain runs from counts or arrivals through calibration and response to a model parameter.
  • A defensible claim states the alternative, the approximation, and the evidence that would change the conclusion.

Observational signature

Ask what the detector actually records: photon energy, arrival time, position, or a particle direction. The source interpretation is credible only when the signature survives background subtraction, calibration choices, and a plausible alternative model.

Before fitting, estimate the scale from the governing relation. Then vary one input by 10 percent and predict the direction of the output change. This catches hidden unit errors and clarifies which uncertainty dominates.
