Interpretation and limits

Lensing shear is a coherent distortion sourced by projected gravitational potential. A 3x2pt analysis combines cosmic shear, galaxy-galaxy lensing, and clustering, while source redshift distributions, intrinsic alignments, and calibration enter the inference.

Boundary: S8= sigma8 sqrt(Omega_m/0.3) compresses a degeneracy direction; it is not a direct measurement of sigma8 alone.

Evidence: The DES Y3 paper is the cited source; the package uses only its published parameter summaries and a clearly synthetic geometry illustration, not shear catalogs or correlation-function vectors.

Active check: Discuss shear calibration, photo-z, intrinsic alignments, baryonic feedback, and survey masks.
