Physical and observational argument

Before baryon drag, pressure in the photon-baryon fluid launches acoustic waves. The maximum propagation distance becomes a preferred comoving separation in later matter clustering. An isotropic analysis averages radial and transverse dilation into D_V, while anisotropic clustering preserves D_M/r_d and H(z)r_d. The ruler's physical length is set by early-universe densities; a late-time distance in Mpc is therefore conditional on its calibration. When using published BOSS summaries, carry the r_d,fid/r_d factor explicitly and do not compare it as an unscaled absolute distance by accident.

The pre-recombination sound horizon imprints a preferred clustering separation. Isotropic analyses compress transverse and radial information into D_V/r_d; anisotropic analyses constrain D_M/r_d and H(z)r_d separately.

Three published BOSS DR12 consensus points at z=0.38, 0.51, and 0.61 are included as literature-summary measurements, not raw clustering data.
