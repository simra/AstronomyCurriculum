Lecture 11: Cross-Probe Concordance and Tension

Driving question: When do two experiments disagree?

Learning objectives

Concordance

Interpretation and limits

A tension claim requires harmonized parameter definitions, assumptions, and uncertainty models. Shared data, calibrations, or theory priors create covariance; scalar error-bar separation is only a diagnostic under strong independence assumptions.

Boundary: Do not calculate a 'sigma tension' by adding published errors in quadrature unless independence and comparable posterior shapes are defensible.
