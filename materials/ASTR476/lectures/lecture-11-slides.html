Quantitative anchor

Probe summaries in the package are not combined because the underlying likelihoods and cross-probe covariance are not included.

\[Delta theta = theta_A - theta_B; significance requires Cov(theta_A,theta_B)\]

Interpretation and limits

A tension claim requires harmonized parameter definitions, assumptions, and uncertainty models. Shared data, calibrations, or theory priors create covariance; scalar error-bar separation is only a diagnostic under strong independence assumptions.

Boundary: Do not calculate a 'sigma tension' by adding published errors in quadrature unless independence and comparable posterior shapes are defensible.

Evidence: All comparison points are literature summaries and are tagged to their exact analysis assumptions in the dataset table and reference log.

Active check: Specify the metadata required before a joint analysis can be run.
