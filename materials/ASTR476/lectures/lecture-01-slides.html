Quantitative anchor

At z=0.1, cz is 29,979.2 km/s as a low-z scale; the expansion interpretation requires the caveat in the lecture.

\[1+z = a(t_0)/a(t_e)\]

Interpretation and limits

Cosmological redshift is a ratio of scale factors, not a Doppler velocity except in the low-redshift limit. Distinguish cosmological redshift from peculiar velocity and gravitational shifts before interpreting a spectrum.

Boundary: At low z, peculiar velocities can be a substantial fraction of cz. Redshift alone is not a distance measurement.

Evidence: Spectroscopic line identification supplies wavelength ratios; the Pantheon+ excerpt provides heliocentric/CMB-frame redshift fields for supernovae.
