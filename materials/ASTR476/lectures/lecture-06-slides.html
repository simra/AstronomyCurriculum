Quantitative anchor

Planck reports 100 theta-star=1.0411 +/- 0.0003; conversion gives theta-star=0.596506 degrees.

\[theta_* = r_s(z_*)/D_M(z_*)\]

Interpretation and limits

Acoustic peak spacing compares the sound horizon at last scattering to its angular-diameter distance. The observed angular scale is precise, but converting it to late-time parameters depends on the pre-recombination model and correlated spectra.

Boundary: The plotted schematic is not Planck data, and theta_* is not simply the angular size of a rigid object with no recombination physics.

Evidence: The official Planck Legacy Archive binned TT/TE products were requested during production but could not be retrieved (TT extraction failure; TE HTTP 401). Only the published parameter summary is used; the plotted peak sequence is explicitly theoretical.
