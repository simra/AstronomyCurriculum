Physical and observational argument

Weak lensing shears background galaxy images through line-of-sight tidal fields. In the thin-lens limit, tangential shear traces the projected surface-density contrast divided by a geometry-dependent critical density. Cosmic shear correlates source shapes; galaxy-galaxy lensing cross-correlates lens positions and source shapes; clustering correlates lens positions. Combining them can calibrate biases but also couples nuisance parameters such as source redshift distributions, intrinsic alignments, shear response, galaxy bias and baryonic structure. S8 compresses a long posterior direction and should be interpreted with its model and covariance, not as sigma8 alone.

Lensing shear is a coherent distortion sourced by projected gravitational potential. A 3x2pt analysis combines cosmic shear, galaxy-galaxy lensing, and clustering, while source redshift distributions, intrinsic alignments, and calibration enter the inference.

The DES Y3 paper is the cited source; the package uses only its published parameter summaries and a clearly synthetic geometry illustration, not shear catalogs or correlation-function vectors.
