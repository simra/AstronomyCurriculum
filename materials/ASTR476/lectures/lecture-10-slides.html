Quantitative anchor

The 18 Pantheon+ redshifts were selected near fixed targets, so their histogram cannot estimate the parent survey completeness.

\[p(detected|x) = S(x) p(x) / integral S(x')p(x') dx'\]

Interpretation and limits

A flux-limited survey samples a population through a selection function. Detection thresholds, cadence, color cuts, spectroscopic follow-up, and analysis cuts shape the observed redshift and luminosity distributions.

Boundary: An observed histogram cannot by itself identify completeness because it mixes cosmological volume, population evolution, and selection.

Evidence: The release documentation and published sample counts are cited; no survey completeness map or detection efficiency curve is bundled.

Active check: Explain forward modeling of selection and why truncating a likelihood after fitting is generally wrong.
