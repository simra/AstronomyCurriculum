Quantitative anchor

The normalized model growth factor is D(0.5)=0.76892 and D(1)=0.60675; these are model outputs.

\[delta'' + (2+H'/H)delta' - 3 Omega_m(a) delta/2 = 0\]

Interpretation and limits

Linear density growth responds to the expansion history and clustering matter. Redshift-space distortions probe coherent peculiar velocities and constrain f sigma8, complementing geometric distance probes.

Boundary: The scale-independent growth equation assumes linear perturbations and general relativity with smooth dark energy; massive neutrinos and modified gravity alter the picture.

Evidence: Growth curves in this package are model calculations, not measured galaxy power spectra; published DES S8 is used only as a separate summary constraint.
