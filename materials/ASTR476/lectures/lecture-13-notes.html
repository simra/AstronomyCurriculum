Physical and observational argument

A useful error budget names where uncertainty originates and how it propagates: detector response, photometric calibration, standardization, redshift, selection, population variation and cosmological modeling. Independent noise often averages down; a shared calibration mode does not. Covariance is a mathematical representation of these dependencies, while nuisance parameters can expose physically interpretable uncertainty. Provenance controls another failure mode: unknown units, reordered rows, stale releases or undocumented transformations. A digest confirms which bytes were analyzed, but only tests, source verification and scientific review establish whether those bytes and assumptions are appropriate.

Precision cosmology depends on calibration chains and nuisance models as much as sample size. An error budget names sources, correlations, mitigation, and residual validation rather than collapsing every uncertainty into one bar.
