Lecture 03: Type Ia Supernovae as Standardizable Candles

Driving question: How does a light curve become a distance indicator?

Learning objectives

Type Ia supernova

Definition: A thermonuclear stellar explosion used as a distance indicator after empirical standardization.

Quantitative anchor

At z=1, the computed distance modulus is 44.1634 mag; 0.01 mag corresponds to about 0.462% in distance.

\[mu = m_B - M_B = 5 log_10(D_L/Mpc)+25\]

Interpretation and limits

A Type Ia supernova is standardized through empirical light-curve and color relations; it is not a perfectly identical explosion. The Hubble diagram constrains relative expansion history while absolute calibration is entangled with the standardized absolute magnitude.
