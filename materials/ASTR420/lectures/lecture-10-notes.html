ASTR 420 Lecture 10: Baryon Acoustic Oscillations

Cosmology · study notes

Learning objectives

  • Use the sound horizon as a standard ruler and identify the role of BAO in geometry tests.
  • Explain photon-baryon sound speed, drag epoch, and correlation peaks using the governing relation.
  • Separate measurement, calibration, inference, and model limitation.

Lecture arc

BAO is not a fixed ruler in physical coordinates; the measured angular and redshift separations are translated through a cosmological model. Begin with the observational puzzle and ask students what is directly measured. Introduce photon-baryon sound speed, drag epoch, and correlation peaks only after the data-model boundary is explicit.

The visual is a reasoning object: students should narrate what each line, region, or axis means before seeing the equation.

Derivation and interpretation

\(r_s=integral_0^ad c_s(a) da / [a^2 H(a)]\)

Define the symbols, units, and assumptions. Explain why the relation is useful and where it fails. Relate the result to the paired lab's data record rather than treating the equation as a detached recipe.

Worked example

A 150 Mpc comoving ruler subtending an angle theta constrains D_M through theta = r_s/D_M; the same ruler in redshift constrains H(z).

The ruler is inferred from a correlation feature and depends on the adopted sound-horizon calibration. Require students to estimate the answer, show units, and state whether the approximation is controlled, empirical, or merely illustrative.

Misconceptions and study guidance

BAO is not a fixed ruler in physical coordinates; the measured angular and redshift separations are translated through a cosmological model. Common traps include confusing redshift with a unique velocity, treating a model-dependent parameter as a direct measurement, and overlooking covariance. Study by reproducing the calculation, changing one assumption, and comparing the result with the exact OpenStax anchor listed in the reference log.

Preparation for the problem set: write a one-paragraph evidence chain from observation to conclusion and identify the weakest link.
