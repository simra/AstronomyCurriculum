ASTR 420 Lecture 03: The Friedmann Equation

Cosmology · study notes

Learning objectives

  • Use the density parameters to identify curvature and expansion regimes.
  • Explain critical density, curvature, and the Friedmann equation using the governing relation.
  • Separate measurement, calibration, inference, and model limitation.

Lecture arc

The equation is a dynamical bookkeeping statement: each component redshifts differently and therefore dominates at a different epoch. Begin with the observational puzzle and ask students what is directly measured. Introduce critical density, curvature, and the Friedmann equation only after the data-model boundary is explicit.

The visual is a reasoning object: students should narrate what each line, region, or axis means before seeing the equation.

Derivation and interpretation

\(H^2(a)=H_0^2[\\Omega_r a^{-4}+\\Omega_m a^{-3}+\\Omega_k a^{-2}+\\Omega_\\Lambda]\)

Define the symbols, units, and assumptions. Explain why the relation is useful and where it fails. Relate the result to the paired lab's data record rather than treating the equation as a detached recipe.

Worked example

rho_crit = 3H0^2/(8 pi G) = 1.260744e+11 solar masses Mpc^-3.

The density is an energy-density scale; Omega values compare each component with this critical value. Require students to estimate the answer, show units, and state whether the approximation is controlled, empirical, or merely illustrative.

Misconceptions and study guidance

The equation is a dynamical bookkeeping statement: each component redshifts differently and therefore dominates at a different epoch. Common traps include confusing redshift with a unique velocity, treating a model-dependent parameter as a direct measurement, and overlooking covariance. Study by reproducing the calculation, changing one assumption, and comparing the result with the exact OpenStax anchor listed in the reference log.

Preparation for the problem set: write a one-paragraph evidence chain from observation to conclusion and identify the weakest link.
