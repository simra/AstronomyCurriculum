# ASTR 360 Datasets

- `ism_phases.csv` - The six real, standard published ISM phase parameters (McKee & Ostriker 1977; Draine 2011 textbook Table 1.1): density, temperature, pressure, and volume filling factor for molecular clouds, the cold/warm neutral medium, the warm/hot ionized medium, and H II regions.
- `real_objects.csv` - Real, standard published quantities for the specific objects used throughout this course's lectures, labs, and problem sets: the 3C 273 and Cygnus X-1 HI/X-ray sightlines, Cygnus OB2 No. 12 and HD 200775's extinction parameters, the Orion and Taurus Molecular Clouds' masses, Barnard 68's mass, the Orion and Rosette Nebulae's ionizing-photon rates, the Cassiopeia A and Tycho supernova-remnant shock velocities, and the Crab Nebula's magnetic-field strength.
