ASTR 360 Problem Set 07: Shocks, Cosmic Rays, and Magnetic Fields

Extend this unit's capstone tools to a second real supernova remnant and additional density/energy regimes.

Problems

1. Tycho's SNR shock 15 points

Using Tycho's real published shock velocity (4,600 km/s), compute its post-shock ion temperature, and compare it to Cassiopeia A's (computed in the lab), stating which remnant's shock produces the hotter predicted plasma and by what percentage.

2. Cosmic-ray flux above the knee 15 points

Using the steeper above-knee power-law index (3.1), compute the cosmic-ray flux at 10x the knee energy (3x10^16 eV) relative to the flux exactly at the knee, and compare the steepness of this decline to the flux decline over the same energy ratio using the below-knee index (2.7) instead.

3. Magnetic field in a less-dense cloud 15 points

Using the Crutcher (2012) B-n relation, compute the predicted magnetic-field strength for gas at Barnard 68's density (8.0e+04 cm^-3, from Unit 5), and compare it to the OMC-1 core's predicted field (computed in the lab), stating which core is predicted to have the stronger field and why, given the two cores' different densities.

4. Synchrotron frequency for a lower-energy electron 15 points

Using the Crab Nebula's real magnetic field, compute the synchrotron critical frequency for a lower-energy electron with Lorentz factor 1.0e4 (rather than the lab's 1.0e6), and identify what part of the electromagnetic spectrum this frequency falls in, connecting your answer to the nu_c ∝ gamma^2 scaling.

Due and Scope

Submit a complete derivation with equations, units, labeled quantities, and a short narrative interpretation for each problem. Show every step; a correct final number without a visible derivation receives partial credit at most, and quoting a lecture number without adapting it to this problem's specific inputs receives no credit for that step.
