# ASTR 360: The Interstellar Medium

3-credit, 300-level elective. Year 3, in the four-year curriculum. Prerequisites: ASTR 310; PHYS 271.

See [syllabus.html](syllabus.html) for the full syllabus, [schedule.html](schedule.html) for the lecture/lab/problem-set schedule, and [index.html](index.html) for the full course package landing page.

## Structure

- `lectures/` - 14 lecture slide decks and paired lecture notes.
- `labs/` - 7 labs (biweekly, matched 1:1 with problem sets).
- `problem-sets/` - 7 problem sets, each with a solution key and assessment instructions.
- `src/` - generator scripts that compute every worked example programmatically and produce the HTML lecture/lab/problem-set content.
- `data/` - course datasets (see `data/README.md`).
- `reference-log.md` - reference, dataset, and visual verification log.
- `review-report.md` - review status and history.

## What This Course Builds On and Adds

ASTR 360 is an upper-division elective on interstellar-medium physics, requiring ASTR 310 (Stellar Astrophysics) and PHYS 271 as prerequisites. It derives, from first principles, the ideal-gas pressure-balance and thermal-instability criteria that sort the ISM into discrete phases, the 21 cm hyperfine radiative-transfer solution and HI column-density formula, the Cardelli extinction-curve parametrization, the CO-to-H2 mass conversion and the virial theorem, the Jeans instability criterion and free-fall collapse time, the Stromgren-sphere ionization-balance derivation and nebular emission-line diagnostics, the Rankine-Hugoniot shock-jump conditions, and the cosmic-ray power-law spectrum, applying each derivation to real, standard published interstellar-medium data: the 3C 273 and Cygnus X-1 HI/X-ray sightlines, the heavily reddened star Cygnus OB2 No. 12, the real Orion and Taurus Molecular Clouds, the classic Bok globule Barnard 68, the Orion and Rosette Nebulae, the Cassiopeia A and Tycho supernova remnants, and the Crab Nebula. Every worked numeric example is computed programmatically from these real datasets rather than hand-typed.
