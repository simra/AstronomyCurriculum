2. Comparison to the Sun

Alpha Centauri A's estimated central pressure (6.999e+13 Pa) is 0.52 times the Sun's (1.345e+14 Pa). Because P_c ∝ M²/R⁴, and Alpha Centauri A has both larger mass (raising P_c) and larger radius (lowering P_c, more strongly since R is raised to the fourth power), the net effect and its sign depend on which scaling dominates; the computed ratio above shows which one wins for these specific values.
