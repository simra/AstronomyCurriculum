ASTR 310 Problem Set 05: Mass-Ratio Splitting and Tidal Effects at Europa

Problem set for Unit 5.

Problems

1. Splitting the Sirius AB total mass 20 points

Using your Kepler-derived Sirius AB total mass from Problem Set 04 (3.079 M⊙) and the independently measured mass ratio M_B/M_A = 0.4935, split the total mass into individual masses for Sirius A and Sirius B.

2. Europa’s Roche limit 15 points

Europa (M = 4.7998e+22 kg, R = 1.5608e+06 m) orbits Jupiter (mean density 1326 kg/m³) at a semimajor axis of 671100 km. Compute Europa's mean density, then its rigid-body Roche limit around Jupiter, and state whether Europa is safely outside its own Roche limit.

3. Europa’s tidal acceleration 20 points

Compute Europa's own surface gravity (g = GM_Europa/R_Europa²) and the differential tidal acceleration across Europa due to Jupiter, and express the tidal acceleration as a percentage of Europa's own surface gravity. Compare your result (as a ratio) to Io's tidal-acceleration-to-surface-gravity percentage from Lecture 10/Lab 05, and state which moon experiences the stronger relative tidal stress and why (consider both distance and radius).

4. Physical interpretation 15 points

Europa, like Io, is held in a slightly eccentric orbit by the same Laplace orbital resonance (with Io and Ganymede). Europa is thought to have a subsurface liquid water ocean, plausibly maintained partly by tidal heating. Using your Problem 3 comparison to Io, explain qualitatively why Europa's tidal heating is expected to be significant but weaker than Io's, consistent with Europa showing an icy (not volcanically resurfaced) surface.

Due and Scope

Submit a complete derivation with equations, units, labeled quantities, and a short narrative interpretation for each problem. Show every step; a correct final number without a visible derivation receives partial credit at most, and quoting a lecture number without adapting it to this problem's specific inputs receives no credit for that step.
