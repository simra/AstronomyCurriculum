ASTR 120 · Lecture 10

Moons, Rings, and Icy Ocean Worlds

Solar-system evidence, physics, and exploration

Why This Question Matters

Voyager and Cassini transformed icy moons from dots into candidate ocean worlds.

Learning Goals

  • Assess evidence for subsurface oceans and active icy-world geology.
  • Connect a mission or observation to the physical interpretation.
  • Use a quantitative or comparative argument with explicit assumptions.

Opening Phenomenon

Moons and rings reveal tidal heating, orbital evolution, and possible subsurface oceans.

Ocean Worlds Need Three Tests

Liquid: magnetic induction, gravity, libration, or plume evidence.

Energy: tidal flexing, radiogenic heat, or chemical disequilibrium.

Exchange: cracks, plumes, resurfacing, or chemistry connecting ocean and surface.

Rings and Moons Share a Dynamical Environment

Rings, resonances, shepherd moons, and tidal heating all arise because gravity is not just a central force in these systems; nearby bodies continually perturb each other.

Evidence We Need

  • Io's volcanoes, Europa's fractures, and Enceladus' plumes all point to tidal energy.
  • Rings are thin, structured, and dynamically young in places.
  • Titan has a dense atmosphere and organic chemistry.

Europa: Ice Shell as Evidence

Europa's fractured surface is interpreted with orbital resonance, tidal stress, induced magnetic field measurements, and surface composition. The image motivates a model; it does not prove an ocean alone.

Tidal Heating Logic

\[ \mathrm{tidal\ heating}\uparrow\quad \mathrm{with}\quad e^2,\ \mathrm{orbital\ forcing},\ \mathrm{and\ internal\ dissipation} \]

Eccentricity keeps the tidal bulge changing through the orbit. Internal friction turns that repeated flexing into heat.

Worked Comparison: Europa and Enceladus

  • Europa: large moon, induced magnetic evidence, young fractured ice, possible plume activity.
  • Enceladus: small moon, confirmed plumes, heat at tiger stripes, Cassini sampled plume material.
  • Both are ocean-world candidates, but Enceladus currently gives more direct material exchange evidence.

Evidence Ranking from the Local Icy-Moon Table

Confirmed plumes, gravity/libration support, and active heat flow make Enceladus a strong exchange case. Europa has stronger scale and induction evidence, while Ganymede and Callisto have ocean clues with weaker surface exchange.

Ocean-World Pitfall: Ice Cracks Are Not Enough

Fractured terrain can form by tectonic stress without requiring a present global ocean. Strong ocean claims need interior, energy, and exchange evidence to agree.

Reasoning Prompt: Choose a Flyby Measurement

A Europa plume is reported but not confirmed. Would you prioritize UV imaging, dust analysis, magnetometry, or thermal mapping on the next flyby? Defend the choice by connecting it to liquid, energy, or exchange.

Synthesis: Habitability Begins with Evidence Architecture

  • Liquid-water claims require interior evidence.
  • Activity claims require an energy source and present-day expression.
  • Habitability becomes stronger when ocean material can reach instruments or the surface.
