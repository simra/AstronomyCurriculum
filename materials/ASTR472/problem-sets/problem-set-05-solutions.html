Problem 1: Radio velocity

Worked solution: v_radio=c Delta-nu/nu_0=105.5 km/s. A 10-kHz channel spans 2.111 km/s. This first-order convention is suitable for Galactic speeds and must be tied to a stated reference frame.

Common errors

  • Converting MHz/GHz or arcseconds/radians incorrectly.
  • Confusing per-beam flux with integrated flux or brightness temperature with kinetic temperature.
  • Treating flux-scale uncertainty as independent for every source.
  • Using a linear Doppler approximation outside its regime or silently changing velocity convention.
  • Calling a synthetic record an observation or reporting a completeness correction without its selection assumptions.
