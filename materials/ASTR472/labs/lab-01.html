ASTR 472 Lab 01: Continuum Mechanisms and Spectral Indices

Lectures 01-02 · radio spectra and antenna response

Learning objectives

  • fit a spectral index to a frequency-flux series and distinguish an optically thin synchrotron slope from a low-frequency turnover.
  • Propagate a statistical or calibration uncertainty and report a limitation.
  • Preserve a reproducible record of inputs, transformations, and outputs.

Preparation, materials, and setup

Use Python 3 with the standard library (or a spreadsheet capable of regression and plotting), the supplied ../data/continuum_spectrum.csv, and the relevant lecture notes. Record file name, row count, columns, units, and provenance level before editing. No facility observing time is assumed.

Dataset status: the supplied compact record is synthetic/instructor-provided (level 3), not an archived observation. Compare its analysis workflow with the real survey or observatory source cited in ../reference-log.md.

Computed check: FWHM = 30.03 arcmin; A_eff = 343.6 m^2.

Apparatus and procedure

  1. Read the CSV schema and plot log flux against log frequency, retaining measurement uncertainties.
  2. Fit S_nu proportional to nu^alpha using weighted least squares; compare a single power law with a broken/turnover model using residuals.
  3. Compute the synchrotron characteristic frequency for the supplied B and gamma values and compare its scale with the sampled band.
  4. Convolve a point-source and an extended-source sketch with the listed single-dish beam; explain beam dilution and flux conservation.
  5. Perturb each flux by its quoted uncertainty in 1000 seeded realizations and report the median alpha and central 68% interval.

Deliverables and criteria

  • Reproducible code or spreadsheet with inputs and units documented.
  • One publication-quality labeled plot and a short interpretation (400-700 words).
  • Uncertainty budget that separates random, common-mode, and model terms where relevant.
  • Credit requires correct method, units, interpretation, provenance, and a limitation; a copied numeric result without an audit trail earns at most half credit.

Interpretive boundary

A single power law is only a local empirical fit; curvature can reflect aging, absorption, or mixed components. State explicitly which parts of your result are facts about this prepared teaching record and which are transferable inference methods.
