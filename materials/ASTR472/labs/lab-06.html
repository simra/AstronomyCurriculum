Apparatus and procedure

  1. Plot arrival-time offset against observing frequency and fit t=t_inf+K DM nu^-2 with uncertainty weights.
  2. Compare fitted DM with the supplied reference DM and report residuals in milliseconds.
  3. Calculate intra-channel dispersive smearing for the listed channel widths at the lowest observing frequency.
  4. Test a pulse-broadening term proportional to nu^-4 and compare residual structure before and after the fit.
  5. Explain why a survey's measured detection fraction depends on pulse width, DM, and cadence.
