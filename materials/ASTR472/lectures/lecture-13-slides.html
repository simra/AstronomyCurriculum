Observation before interpretation

A 21-cm velocity cube turns a line-of-sight Doppler field into a model-dependent map of Galactic gas and kinematics.

  • The 21-cm line is observable through much of the Galactic disk, including dusty sightlines.
  • For a flat circular rotation curve, v_LSR=(Theta(R)R0/R-Theta0)sin(l) on the inner-Galaxy sightline.
  • Streaming motions, spiral arms, and the solar motion perturb the simple axisymmetric conversion.

Keep four layers distinct in your notebook: raw correlation or spectrum, calibrated product, model parameter, and physical claim.

Physical model

Set Theta(R)=Theta0 and solve v_LSR=Theta0(R0/R-1)sin(l) for R=R0/[1+v_LSR/(Theta0 sin(l))]. This gives Galactocentric radius for a chosen longitude under circular rotation. Heliocentric distance requires a second geometric step and has a near/far ambiguity inside the solar circle.

\(R=\frac{R_0}{1+v_{\rm LSR}/(\Theta_0\sin l)}\)

Define each quantity and convention before substitution. A relation is useful only inside its stated regime.
