Worked Solutions and Rubric Notes

1. Flat Rotation Curve

If v is constant, M(r) is proportional to r. Doubling radius requires roughly doubling enclosed mass. If visible matter fades at large radius, continued mass increase suggests unseen mass such as dark matter.

3. Hubble-Law Distance

d = v/H0 = 7000/70 = 100 Mpc. Imperfections include peculiar velocities, measurement uncertainty, local gravitational motions, and limitations of the linear Hubble law at larger redshift.
