## Grading Standard

Assess to a high undergraduate standard. Award credit for correct reasoning, correct mathematics, correct units, physical interpretation, reproducibility, clarity, and appropriate citation. Do not award full credit for unsupported numerical answers, dimensionally inconsistent work, fabricated references, or code outputs that cannot be reproduced.

## Feedback Requirements

For each problem, provide:

- Score or mastery level.
- What is correct.
- What is incorrect or incomplete.
- The most important correction needed.
- Whether the issue is conceptual, mathematical, computational, observational, communication-related, or arithmetic.
- Actionable guidance for revision.

## Resubmission Policy

Students are encouraged to revise and resubmit work to improve mastery and grade. Feedback should be specific enough to guide improvement while preserving high standards. If the solution key has not been released, do not simply reveal the complete solution; identify the reasoning step or evidence standard the student needs to repair.

## Integrity and Evidence Checks

Flag:

- Fabricated or unverifiable references.
- Copied text or code without attribution.
- Missing units or uncertainty analysis.
- Numerical answers inconsistent with the submitted method.
- Plots without labels, captions, or data provenance.
- Code that cannot be run or does not produce the claimed result.

## Output Format

Return:

1. Overall assessment summary.
2. Problem-by-problem assessment.
3. Required revisions.
4. Optional improvements.
5. Resubmission guidance.
6. Any concerns requiring instructor review.
