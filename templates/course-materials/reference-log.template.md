## Verification Rules

- Verified sources must be traceable through a publisher, DOI, arXiv record, library record, official mission/observatory page, documentation page, or public archive.
- Candidate references must be labeled `NEEDS VERIFICATION` and must not appear as final citations.
- Rejected references should briefly state why they were rejected.
- Visual credits must identify the creator or institution, source page, license or usage note when available, and the artifact where the visual appears.
